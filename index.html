How to Use

Short guides for everything in GRAND GARAGE. Tap a topic to see the steps.

Add a VehicleYour first car or motorcycle in a minute.
  1. On the home screen, tap My Vehicles at the bottom.
  2. Tap + and choose Add Car or Add Motorcycle.
  3. Pick the make and model from the lists, or type your own, then tap Save.
  4. Tap a vehicle in My Vehicles to open its page.
PhotosTake photos, choose a cover and play a slideshow.
  1. Open a vehicle and choose the Photos tab.
  2. Tap + to take a photo or choose photos from your library.
  3. Tap a photo to see it full screen. Double-tap to zoom, swipe down to close.
  4. In the full-screen view, the star makes the photo the cover and the heart adds it to your favourites.
  5. Tap ▶ to play a slideshow with music.
Details & ScanningLet the camera fill in your papers.
  1. Open a vehicle and choose the Details tab.
  2. Tap Scan Registration Certificate and hold the paper in front of the camera. VIN, plate, engine and more are filled in for you.
  3. Tap Scan Insurance Policy to fill in the insurance the same way.
  4. Check the result, then tap Save. Tap Edit at any time to change something.
Dates & RemindersNever miss a service, inspection, tax or insurance date.
  1. In the Details tab, tap Edit and enter the dates: inspection, road tax, insurance expiry and next service.
  2. GRAND GARAGE reminds you 30 and 7 days before, then every day in the last days.
  3. The nearest date always shows under Next Up on the home screen.
  4. Allow notifications when asked, otherwise reminders cannot appear.
Service History & CostsEvery service, repair and fuel stop, with totals.
  1. Open a vehicle and choose the History tab.
  2. Tap Add Entry for a service, repair, tyres or parts. Tap Fuel for a quick fuel entry.
  3. Add the date, mileage, cost and service centre, and a photo of the receipt.
  4. At the top you see the total spent, the cost per year and per km or mile.
FilesInvoices and papers, always with the vehicle.
  1. Open a vehicle and choose the Files tab.
  2. Tap Add Files to add PDFs and documents, or Scan Document to use the camera.
  3. Tap a file to open it, or share it from there.
Share & SellA photo card, a PDF dossier or a complete sale package.
  1. Open a vehicle, choose the Details tab and tap the share button at the top right.
  2. Share Card: an elegant picture of your vehicle for Instagram or WhatsApp.
  3. Vehicle Dossier (PDF): specifications, history and photos in one document.
  4. Prepare for Sale: everything a buyer wants to see. Your purchase price and personal details are never included.
Backup & New iPhoneKeep your garage safe in iCloud Drive.
  1. On the home screen, tap … and then Settings.
  2. Tap Backup Folder and choose a folder in iCloud Drive, then turn on Daily Automatic Backup.
  3. On a new iPhone or iPad, install GRAND GARAGE, open Settings and tap Restore from Backup.
  4. Important: without a backup, deleting the app also deletes your garage.
Widgets & SiriYour vehicles and next date on the Home Screen.
  1. Touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for GRAND GARAGE.
  2. Choose the photo widget or Next Up and tap Add Widget.
  3. Say “Hey Siri, add fuel in GRAND GARAGE” or “What’s next in GRAND GARAGE”.
Make It YoursBackground, app icon, privacy lock and favourites.
  1. Settings › Home Screen: choose the background and your name.
  2. Settings › App Icon: Navy Gold, Black Gold, Platinum or Rose Gold.
  3. Settings › Lock Details, Files & History: protect them with Face ID.
  4. Tap the heart on a vehicle to make it a favourite, and search by name, plate or VIN in My Vehicles.
